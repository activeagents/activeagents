## Release steps (the 1.9.3–1.9.5 recipe)

1. Worktree from `origin/main`; bump `lib/active_agent/version.rb`,
   `actionagent/lib/action_agent/version.rb`, `browser-sidecar/package.json`
   and its lock; rename `## [Unreleased]` to `## [1.9.6] - <date>` with a
   short preamble.
2. Release PR against `main`; wait for the 12 checks; merge (no auto-merge
   on the repo).
3. `git tag -a v1.9.6 -m "activeagent and actionagent 1.9.6"` on the merge
   commit; push the tag. The Release workflow publishes RubyGems, npm and the
   ghcr sidecar image.
4. Host repin (below), staging smoke test, production dispatch.

## Host side, after the gems are cut

## Staging check for the phone layouts

#654 was verified against fixtures in a headless browser, not a live
dashboard. On staging, at 360×780, 390×844 and 768×1024:

1. `/dashboard`: the sidebar is a drawer behind the header's menu button;
   it closes on the backdrop, the close button, Escape and navigation.
2. `/dashboard/interactions`, `/dashboard/traces`: no horizontal page
   scroll; stat tiles 2×2; meta strips folded into the cards.
3. `/dashboard/agents/<id>`: one pane at a time, "≡ Runs" / "Run #N →"
   switch; the composer sticks to the bottom.
4. `/dashboard/evaluations`: the runs × scenarios matrix scrolls inside its
   box with a sticky first column; the open scenario's drill-down is the
   width of the scrollport.
5. Dark theme at 360 and 768 on the three views above, and the agent
   interactions page once #628 carries its dark pass.
